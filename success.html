Have these handy for your intake

  • Your current medication list, including supplements
  • The name and location of your preferred pharmacy
  • A recent weight, and any labs from the past year if you have them
  • Your insurance card, if you want us to check GLP-1 coverage

Once you're a patient, your patient portal is also how you message the practice between visits — secure, straight to Dr. Merrick and your chart, answered within one business day. Never use messaging for an emergency: call 911.
