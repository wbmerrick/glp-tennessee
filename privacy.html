Privacy Policy

Effective date: [TO SET AT LAUNCH]

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how this website, operated by William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC ("the Practice"), handles information. Your medical information is not collected through this website. Once you become a patient, your health information is collected and maintained in the Practice's HIPAA-compliant electronic health record and patient portal, and is governed by the Notice of Privacy Practices you receive there — not by this website policy.

2. The eligibility checker stays in your browser

The eligibility check on our homepage (residency, age, height, weight, and screening questions) runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not transmitted to us, stored, or shared — they exist only on your screen and disappear when you close the page.

3. Information the website does handle

  • Payment information. Membership payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes directly to Stripe and is never seen or stored by the Practice. Stripe provides us your name, email, and payment status so we can manage your membership. Stripe's handling of your data is described in its own privacy policy.
  • Contact information. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. Please do not send detailed medical information by regular email; use the patient portal once enrolled.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

4. How we use information

To operate the website, process and manage memberships, respond to inquiries, meet legal obligations, and protect against fraud and abuse. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

5. Service providers

We share information only with vendors who help us operate — payment processing (Stripe), website hosting, email, and, once you are a patient, our electronic health record vendor — each limited to what they need to perform their service.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the kind of information involved, and retain website-related information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Patient records are retained according to Tennessee medical-record requirements.

7. Children

This website and the Practice's services are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices

You may ask what website-related information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by contacting us below. Requests concerning your medical record are handled under HIPAA through the patient portal.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted here with its effective date.
