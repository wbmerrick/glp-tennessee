Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how this website, operated by William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC ("the Practice"), handles information. Your medical information is not collected through this website. Once you become a patient, your health information is collected and maintained in the Practice's HIPAA-compliant electronic health record and patient portal, and is governed by the Notice of Privacy Practices you receive there — not by this website policy. The eligibility check on our homepage asks a few health-related questions, but they are processed only in your browser and never reach us (Section 2).

2. The eligibility checker stays in your browser

The eligibility check on our homepage (residency, age, height, weight, and screening questions) runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not transmitted to us, stored, or shared — they exist only on your screen and disappear when you close the page.

3. Information the website does handle

  • Payment information. Membership payments are processed by Stripe. Your card number goes directly to Stripe and is never seen or stored by the Practice. Stripe provides us your name, email, and payment status so we can manage your membership. Stripe's handling of your data is described in its own privacy policy.
  • Contact information. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. Please do not send detailed medical information by regular email; use the patient portal once enrolled.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability, and keeps them only for a limited period.
  • Cookies and analytics. This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts, and sets no tracking cookies. Stripe's checkout page and the patient portal set their own cookies when you use them, under their own privacy policies.

4. How we use information

To operate the website, process and manage memberships, respond to inquiries, meet legal obligations, and protect against fraud and abuse. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

5. Service providers

We share information only with vendors who help us operate — payment processing (Stripe), website hosting (Vercel), email, and, once you are a patient, Akute Health, the HIPAA-compliant electronic health record that runs our intake forms, scheduling, and patient portal — each limited to what they need to perform their service.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the kind of information involved. Membership and payment records are kept for as long as you are a member and afterward as long as tax, billing, and legal requirements demand; email correspondence is kept only as long as it is needed to respond to you. Patient records are retained according to Tennessee medical-record requirements (at least ten years from your last visit). If a security breach affects your information, we will notify you as HIPAA and Tennessee law require.

7. Children

This website and the Practice's services are for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your choices

You may ask what website-related information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by contacting us below. Requests concerning your medical record are handled under HIPAA through the patient portal.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted here with its effective date.
