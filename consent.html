Telehealth Informed Consent

Reference copy — you will review and sign this consent in the patient portal during intake. Effective date: [TO SET AT LAUNCH]

1. What telehealth is

Telehealth means receiving medical evaluation and treatment from a physician through secure video, audio, and electronic messaging rather than in person. Your care through GLP Tennessee is provided by William B. Merrick, DO, through William B. Merrick, DO, PLLC, to patients located in the State of Tennessee.

2. Expected benefits

Access to physician care without travel, easier scheduling, consistent monthly follow-up, and continuity with a single physician who knows your history.

3. Possible risks and limitations

  • A telehealth visit does not include a hands-on physical examination, which in some situations can limit what the physician can detect or safely treat remotely.
  • Technology can fail — video or audio may be interrupted, delayed, or of poor quality. If a connection fails, the visit will be completed or rescheduled by phone or another secure method.
  • As with any electronic system, security protocols can in rare cases be breached despite safeguards.
  • The physician may determine during any visit that your condition requires in-person evaluation, laboratory testing, or referral, and will direct you accordingly. Following that direction is part of safe treatment.

4. Alternatives

You may seek the same evaluation and treatment in person from a physician or clinic of your choice. Choosing telehealth is voluntary.

5. Prescriptions

All prescribing decisions are made solely in the physician's independent clinical judgment after evaluation. Membership does not entitle you to any particular medication. Prescriptions, when issued, are transmitted to the licensed pharmacy you designate, and medication is purchased by you directly from that pharmacy.

6. Not for emergencies

Telehealth through this practice is not appropriate for emergencies. If you experience a medical emergency — including but not limited to chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe abdominal pain, or symptoms of a severe allergic reaction — call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

7. Confidentiality and records

Your health information is protected under HIPAA and Tennessee law and is maintained in the Practice's electronic health record. You will receive the Practice's Notice of Privacy Practices in the patient portal, and you have the right to access copies of your records.

8. Your rights and responsibilities

  • You may withhold or withdraw this consent at any time without affecting your right to future care or treatment.
  • You agree to provide accurate, complete, and current information about your health, medications, allergies, and location, and to be physically located in Tennessee during visits.
  • You agree to participate in scheduled follow-up visits, which are a condition of ongoing prescribing — dose changes and refills require monitoring to be done safely.
  • You are responsible for maintaining a relationship with a local primary care provider or, if you do not have one, telling us so we can help you plan for care this practice does not provide.

9. Fees

The membership fee covers professional services as described in the Terms of Use. The Practice does not bill insurance for visits. Medication, pharmacy, and laboratory charges are separate and are paid by you directly to those providers.
