Who qualifies

The basics, before you spend a dollar.

GLP-1 medications are FDA-indicated for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27+ with a weight-related condition like high blood pressure, prediabetes, sleep apnea, or high cholesterol. The eligibility check takes about a minute and nothing you enter is saved.

  • Tennessee resident. You'll need to be physically in Tennessee for visits.
  • 18 or older
  • BMI 30+, or 27+ with a weight-related health condition
  • Not pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning pregnancy
  • No personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2
  • Full history reviewed by the doctor. Conditions like pancreatitis or gastroparesis get an individual look, not an automatic no.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Something else? Email care@glptennessee.com.

Does the $99 include the medication?

No, and that's on purpose. You buy the medication from your own pharmacy at their price, with zero markup from us. Depending on your insurance and the option you choose, members typically pay anywhere from a modest copay to a few hundred dollars a month cash through the manufacturers' direct programs. Dr. Merrick will help you find the cheapest legitimate route for your situation.

Will my insurance cover any of this?

The membership itself is cash-pay. We don't bill insurance for visits, which is how the price stays simple. The medication is a different story: if your plan covers GLP-1s, your pharmacy bills your insurance like any other prescription, and we handle the prior-authorization paperwork as part of your membership.

Is paying the membership a guarantee I'll get a prescription?

No. You're paying for medical care from a physician, and Dr. Merrick only prescribes when it's medically appropriate. That's what makes this a medical practice and not a vending machine. If after your evaluation treatment isn't right for you, he'll tell you straight and refund your first month in full.

Which pharmacy can I use?

Any licensed pharmacy you choose: the one down the road, a big chain, or the manufacturers' own direct-to-patient programs (LillyDirect for Zepbound®, NovoCare for Wegovy®), which often have the best cash prices. The prescription is yours; where you fill it is your call.

How do the visits work?

By secure video, from anywhere in Tennessee. Your first visit is a real evaluation: history, goals, risks, and a plan. After that, monthly check-ins keep your dose titration on schedule and catch side effects early. You can message the practice between visits.

How do I message the practice between visits?

Through your secure patient portal, the same place you completed your intake. Portal messages are encrypted, go straight to Dr. Merrick, and become part of your chart. We respond within one business day. Please don't send medical questions by regular email or text. They aren't secure, and we'll just redirect you to the portal.

Messaging is never for emergencies. If you think you're having one, call 911.

What are the side effects?

The common ones are digestive (nausea, constipation, sometimes vomiting or reflux), mostly during dose increases, and usually manageable by titrating slowly, which is exactly what the monthly follow-ups are for. Serious risks are uncommon but real: pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, and a thyroid-tumor warning from rodent studies, which is why the thyroid history question matters. Dr. Merrick goes through all of it with you before anything is prescribed.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, from your billing portal, no phone call required. Your membership runs through the end of the period you've paid for. If you're on medication, Dr. Merrick will talk with you about how to stop or taper sensibly, so please don't just disappear on him.

Why Tennessee only?

Because that's where Dr. Merrick is licensed and lives. Telehealth law requires your doctor to be licensed in the state where you are. And frankly, a practice that serves one state well beats one that serves fifty states thinly.
